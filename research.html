Dish Forecasting with Set-Based Neural Networks

Forecasting demand for a dish requires considering the other dishes available on the same menu. Since menus vary across dates and locations, they are naturally represented as unordered sets rather than fixed-size vectors or independent time series.

Problem

Predict dish-level choice rates for changing menus across multiple restaurant sites and dates.

Approach

Compare simple baselines with permutation-invariant neural architectures, including DeepSets and attention-based models.

Hidden Markov Models for Volatility Regime Detection

Financial time series often alternate between periods of stability and periods of high uncertainty. This project studies Hidden Markov Models as a probabilistic framework for identifying latent market regimes and modelling changes in volatility over time.

Problem

Identify hidden volatility regimes in financial time series and analyse how market dynamics evolve between calm and stressed periods.

Approach

Implement and study the main HMM algorithms, including Forward for likelihood computation, Viterbi for state decoding, and Baum–Welch for parameter estimation.
