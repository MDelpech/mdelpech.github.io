Hidden Markov Models for Financial Regime Detection

This research project explores the use of Hidden Markov Models to detect latent market regimes in financial time series. I implemented the Forward-Backward, Viterbi, and Baum-Welch algorithms from scratch using Python and NumPy, then applied the model to synthetic data and 20 years of VIX observations to identify volatility regimes and analyse their relationship with periods of market stress.
